Hi! I am Maryam Tavasoli. I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering (Artificial Intelligence and Robotics) from Shahrood University of Technology and a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Ferdowsi University of Mashhad. I have worked for several years as a software and AI engineer, and my research interests are computer vision, machine learning and explainable AI.

In this internship at JdeRobot / RoboticsLab URJC, supervised by Prof. José María Cañas and Dr. Luis Bote-Curiel, I work on deep reinforcement learning for robot pick-and-place in MuJoCo and on explaining the resulting policies.

Welcome! This is the weekly log of my research internship at [JdeRobot](https://jderobot.github.io/) / RoboticsLab URJC (2026), supervised by Prof. José María Cañas and Dr. Luis Bote-Curiel.
The topic is **machine learning + explainability**: train a robot arm to pick and place objects with deep reinforcement learning in [MuJoCo](https://mujoco.org/), and then explain what the learned policy does and why.
Code lives in the [repository](https://github.com/TheRoboticsClub/2026-internship-Maryam_Tavasoli); the posts below track the progress week by week.
